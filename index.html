The whole production.
From one sentence.

You write one sentence. Behind it, Animave runs every step a studio would, and shows you each one, with the time left, while it works.

01

Script

Your idea becomes a script with a hook, a clear structure and lines written for the format.

02

Voice

A natural voice, or a whole cast, reads it with the pace and the mood each line needs.

03

Animation

Claude animates every frame in code, timed to the word it belongs to.

04

Frame check

Key frames are reviewed and fixed before rendering: nothing cut off, nothing on top of the text.

05

Sound

Music under the voice and a sound effect on every action, mixed to streaming loudness.

06

Delivery

A finished 1080 × 1920 reel and a caption to post, ready for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

Questions,
answered.

How long does a reel take?

Most reels are ready in 4 to 12 minutes, depending on the format: 3D formats take the longest. While it works you see every step and an estimate of the time left.

How do credits work?

Each format has a fixed price in credits that you see before you start: an Explainer is 200 credits, a Premium Faceless reel 300, a Survival Story 600. If a reel fails to start, the credits go back to you. Credits never expire.

Who owns the videos I make?

You do. All videos generated on Animave are fully owned by you and can be used commercially without restriction.

Can I use my own footage?

Yes, in Caption News (optional) and Sports Telestrator (your match clip). Only upload footage you have the rights to use. When Caption News uses archive footage, it is openly licensed and credited on screen.

Which formats are there?

Fourteen: Premium Faceless, Explainer, Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon, Caption News, Survival Story, Whiteboard Explainer, Exploded Teardown, Sports Telestrator, Paper-cut Lightbox, Block Horror, 3D Build, 3D Health, Motion Graphics and 3D Render.

Which languages?

Reels are written and voiced in English.

Vertical or horizontal?

Every reel is vertical 9:16 in 1080p, made for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

What happens if I run out of credits?

You can buy a credit pack at any time without changing your plan. Credits are added instantly.
