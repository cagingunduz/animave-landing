Questions,
answered.

How long does a video take?

Most short videos are ready in a few minutes. Longer stories take longer, because every scene is written, voiced, animated and edited; you can follow each scene as it finishes.

How do credits work?

Each format has its own price in credits, and you see it before you start: a 15-second Motion Graphics video is 465 credits, a one-minute Whiteboard video 180. Credits never expire.

Who owns the videos I make?

You do. All videos generated on Animave are fully owned by you and can be used commercially without restriction.

Can I use my own face or someone else's likeness?

You can upload a photo to create a character based on your likeness. You are responsible for having the right to use any likeness you upload. Uploaded photos are not stored after the generation is complete.

Which formats and styles are there?

Seven formats: Storytelling, Motion Graphics, Animated Story, Whiteboard, 2D Animation, Fruit Drama and 3D Render. Visual styles include Western Cartoon, Anime, Pixar-style 3D, Retro and Realistic.

Can I make videos in other languages?

Yes. Voices speak 29 languages. Write the script or the dialogue in any of them and the selected voice reads it naturally.

Vertical or horizontal?

Both. Make 9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, or 16:9 for YouTube.

What happens if I run out of credits?

You can buy a credit pack at any time without changing your plan. Credits are added instantly.
