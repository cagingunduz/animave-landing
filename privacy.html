Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

This policy explains what Animave (“we”, “us”) collects when you use animave.com and app.animave.com (the “Service”), why, who processes it for us, and the choices you have. Questions: animave@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, your name, and, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile Google shares with us (name, email address, profile picture). Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored in readable form.
  • What you give us to make a video: the topics, scripts, settings and choices you enter, and any clips or photos you upload.
  • What we make for you: scripts, voice recordings, animation code, frames and the finished videos, plus their titles and status in your library.
  • Credits and plan: your credit balance, plan and the credit transactions for each video.
  • Technical data: the information browsers and servers exchange to run a website (IP address, browser type, timestamps, error logs).

How we use it

  • To create the videos you ask for and keep them in your library.
  • To run your account: sign-in, credits, plans and support.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To tell you about your account or important changes. We do not send marketing email without your consent.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your uploads or videos to train AI models.

Who processes data for us

We use service providers that process data on our behalf, only to provide the Service:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in and our database.
  • Google: “Continue with Google” sign-in; Gemini models for some image and video steps.
  • Vercel and Railway: hosting of the website, the app and our servers.
  • Cloudflare R2: storage of uploads and finished videos.
  • Anthropic (Claude): writing scripts and the animation code from your topic and settings.
  • OpenAI: speech-to-text to time captions to the narration.
  • ElevenLabs: some voices and sound effects.
  • Replicate and fal.ai: image and video generation in some formats.

Some formats can use openly licensed archive footage (for example Wikimedia Commons or NASA). Fetching it sends only search words, never your personal data. If you buy a paid plan or credits, payments are handled by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data and videos while your account is open. Uploaded clips and photos are kept so your videos can be made and re-made; you can ask us to delete them at any time. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data and files within 30 days, except what we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

Cookies and storage

We use cookies and your browser’s storage only to keep you signed in and remember choices such as “Remember me”. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, and object to or restrict its use, by writing to animave@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The Service is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you by email or in the app when the change is significant.
